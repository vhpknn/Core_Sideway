# RangeBot User Guide (strategy 1.0.0 on Core v1.1)

## 1. Overview
RangeBot trades reversals at the edges of a price range on XAUUSD H1. Flow: detect the market regime > find and lock a range > evaluate a signal at the range edge on each new bar > open a trade with ATR-based SL/TP > manage the trade until it closes. Tested so far only in the Strategy Tester on a demo account; not yet tested on a live demo or live account.

## 2. Requirements
- Core Protect must be off: `InpRRPercent=0`, `InpUseTrailingTP=false`, `InpBreakEvenMode=BE_OFF` (otherwise the EA refuses to start).
- `InpCutLossPoints=3000` (must be wider than the strategy SL, or signals are blocked with SL_TOO_FAR) and `InpMaxSpreadPoints=45` for XAUUSD on FBS Demo.
- Lot size comes from the Core Virtual Capital Auto-Lot, not from a risk percentage.
- Use the `.set` files in `Strategies\RangeBot\v1.1\Set\` (sets 00 to 10) generated by `tools\make_rangebot_sets.py`.

## 3. Getting started
1. Compile `RangeBot_EA_EN.mq5` (English labels) or `RangeBot_EA_TH.mq5` (Thai labels).
2. Attach the EA to an XAUUSD H1 chart and load a `.set` (start with `00_baseline`).
3. Check the Journal for `Protect ของ Core ถูกปิด...` and `เริ่มระบบสำเร็จ` (log text is in Thai).

## 4. How it works
| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Regime | ADX, Bollinger width and EMAs classify RANGE / NEUTRAL / TREND_UP / TREND_DOWN (2-bar confirmation) |
| Range | 30 bars (latest closed bar excluded), height 2 to 6 ATR, at least 2 touches per edge; locked until a close beyond 0.5 ATR outside the range or 30 bars |
| Signal | ZONE (price in the 25% edge zone + reversal candle) or SWEEP (wick beyond the edge, close back inside) + trigger (RSI/STOCH/CANDLE/NONE) + filters |
| Entry | Must pass the loss cap (`InpCutLossPoints`) and RR to TP1 of at least 1.2; a signal waits at most 15 minutes |
| Management | TP1 reached > SL to break-even + 0.1 ATR > hand-off when a bar closes outside the range (Chandelier trail) or timeout 24 bars after TP1 > MaxHold before TP1 (off by default) |
| TREND SHIFT | Before TP1, if the trend goes against the trade: KEEP (default) / TIGHTEN / CLOSE |
| Cooldown | After the manager closes a trade, no new trade until the end of that bar |

## 5. Commonly tuned inputs
| Group | Input | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| R2 | InpRbAdxRangeMax / TrendMin | 20 / 25 | Highest ADX still RANGE / lowest ADX counted as TREND |
| R3 | InpRbRangeLookback, MinTouches | 30, 2 | Bars used to build the range, touches per edge |
| R4 | InpRbRangeDirection | FOLLOW_HTF | Follow the H4 EMA200 bias, or BOTH_EDGES |
| R5 | InpRbEntryType, ZoneEntryPct | BOTH, 25 | ZONE / SWEEP / BOTH, zone depth |
| R6 | InpRbTriggerType | RSI | RSI / STOCH / CANDLE / NONE |
| R7 | InpRbUseAdxSlope, UseAtrVolFilter, UsePercentB | true, true, false | Extra filters |
| R8 | InpRbZoneSlAtr, MinRrTp1, UseTp2 | 1.0, 1.2, true | SL beyond the edge, minimum RR, broker TP = TP2 |
| R9 | InpRbSignalWaitMinutes, BeBufferAtr, UseMidRangeBe | 15, 0.1, false | Signal lifetime, break-even offset, move SL at mid-range |
| R9 | InpRbUseHandOff, ChandelierPeriod/Mult | true, 22/3.0 | Hand-off and trailing |
| R9 | InpRbUseRangeTimeout, RangeTimeoutBars | true, 24 | Close after TP1 if the range does not break |
| R9 | InpRbOnTrendShiftPreTp1 | KEEP | Action when the trend turns against the trade before TP1 |
| R9 | InpRbUseMaxHold, MaxHoldBars | false, 48 | Close trades held too long without reaching TP1 |

## 6. Panel buttons
- RESET RANGE: unlock the range and detect a new one; does not close trades or touch capital.
- BE NOW: move the open trade's SL to break-even (only if better than the current SL and far enough from price for the broker's stop level).

## 7. Reading the Journal
| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SIGNAL BUY/SELL ZONE/SWEEP` | A signal was created and is waiting for entry conditions |
| `SIGNAL WAIT | <reason>` | Waiting (SL_TOO_FAR, RR_LOW, WAIT_SPREAD ...) |
| `SIGNAL EXPIRED` / `SIGNAL DROPPED | COOLDOWN` | Signal timed out / dropped because of the post-close cooldown |
| `ENTRY` / `SL/TP SET` | Trade opened / SL and TP set |
| `TP1 HIT`, `MID-BE`, `HAND-OFF`, `TRAIL` | Trade management events |
| `TIMEOUT`, `MAXHOLD`, `TREND SHIFT` | Early close or adjustment conditions |
| `TRADE CLOSED` | Trade closed, with its state and bars held |

## 8. Limitations
- No partial close (Core closes whole positions); at TP1 the SL only moves to break-even.
- Saving/restoring state via Global Variables does not run in the Strategy Tester and has not been tested on a demo account yet.
- The Tester simulates a random execution delay of several seconds; an SL modification can fail with Invalid stops, which the EA handles.
- Current results contain few trades (short real-tick window), so strategy profitability cannot be concluded. See `docs/work/TEST_MATRIX.md`.
